## GKS (Global Korea Scholarship) Program
This is a government-invited scholarship degree program aimed at fostering global talent and establishing international networks by inviting outstanding students from around the world.The Korean government provides international students with opportunities to study and offers various scholarship programs.
  • Degree Programs
  • Korea-Japan Programs
  • Exchange Students Program
  • GKS Notice

### Programs

#### Program Objectives
South Korea's representative scholarship program that aims to attract talented sudnets worldwide to support their pursuit of degrees and more.

#### Scholarship information
Korean language training + degree programs

#### Selection Quota
Approximately 500 undergraduates (including associate degrees) + 1,900 graduate students

#### Scholarship Duration
Korean language training + associate degrees (2-3 years), bachelor's degrees (4-6 years), master's degrees (2 years), doctoral degrees (3 years)

#### Scholarships Benefits
Airfare, language training costs, tuition, and study allowances
  • Undergraduate Degrees (including Associate Degree)
  • Graduate Degrees (Master's and Doctoral Degrees)

### Programs
Consisting of departments in various fields established over 80 GKS Universities nationwide

#### General Program
Covering various fields, such as humanities, social studies, natural sciences, and arts and physical education, etc.

#### Specialization (UIC)
Science and engineering departments focused on customized curriculum and field experience through industry-university collaboration* Available only for associate and bachelor's degree programs

### Eligibility Criteria
  • Nationality: Applicant and both parents must hold non-Korean citizenship
  • Age: Under 25 years old as of applicable year
  • Education : Associate Degree - High school graduate, Bachelor's Degree - High school or associate degree graduate
  • Academic Performance: Cumulative GPA of 80% or higher in the most recent educational program

### Korea-Japan Joint Higher Education Student Exchange Program
This program aims to foster talent who will serve as bridges between Korea and Japan, leading the future-oriented bilateral relations and promoting friendship and
												cooperation through student exchange.
  • Bachelor’s Degree (Including Associate Degree)
  • Graduate Programs (Master's, Doctoral)

### Scholarship Program for Japanese Nationals

#### Korea-Japan Joint Higher Education Student Exchange Program
Structure: Bachelor’s 1-Year Program, Bachelor’s Short-Term Program

#### Bachelor’s 1-Year Program
  • Japanese nationality
  • Ages 18–30
  • Currently enrolled as a second-year or higher undergraduate student at a Japanese university

#### Bachelor’s Short-Term Program
  • Japanese nationality
  • Ages 18–30
  • Currently enrolled as an undergraduate student at a Japanese university

#### Korea-Japan Future Talent Invitational Program
Selection and Support for Bachelor’s and Associate Degree Scholarship Students
  • 1-year Korean language training +
  • 4–6 years for bachelor’s degree
  • 2–3 years for associate degree

### Scholarship Program for Korean Nationals

#### Korea-Japan Student Exchange Program
Selection and Support for Excellent Korean Exchange Students at Domestic Universities with Student Exchange Agreements with Japanese Universities

#### Korea-Japan Joint Higher Education Student Exchange Program
Similar to the Korea-Japan Joint Higher Education Student Exchange Program (Japanese Scholarship Selection), this program consists of graduate courses, a one-year undergraduate course, and short-term undergraduate courses. ### GKS Exchange Students Program
Supports the expansion of international student exchange to enhance the international competitiveness of domestic universities, while providing exchange students
												with opportunities to understand Korean education and culture.

#### Scholarship Duration
4 months (1 semester)

#### Selection Quota
Approximately 500 international students (subject to budget changes)

#### Scholarship Benefits

#### Scholarship Benefits

#### Eligibility Requirements
  • Undergraduate and graduate (Master's and Doctoral degree) students of foreign nationality who are enrolled in an overseas university that have agreements with a Korean university affiliated with NIIED.
  • Students must be enrolled in a degree exchagne program (those enrolled solely in Korean language courses are excluded).
  • Students whose overall academic performance at their home university is 80% or above.
  • Students who have previously received scholarship from the korean government are not eligible.
  • Dual nationals who hold Korean citizenship are not eligible.
  • Students currently stuying in Korea are not eligible.
  • Studnets cannot apply to more than Korean university at the same time.
  • ※ Scholarship recipients who have completed one semester (4 months) are not eligible to apply for a scholarship extension.
